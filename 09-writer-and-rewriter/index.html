Writer & Rewriter — on-device drafting

Generate text with Chrome's built-in Writer and reshape it with Rewriter (Gemini Nano), running on-device. Lesson: Writer & Rewriter.

Heads up: these two APIs are still behind Chrome flags and are off by default, so on most machines they report unavailable. Enable #writer-api-for-gemini-nano and #rewriter-api-for-gemini-nano in chrome://flags, then restart Chrome. If they're off, each panel below tells you which flag to flip.
